System Integration

How the Systems Work Together

Axonix does not just install devices. We build the cabling, network, security, AV, and lighting layers as one connected infrastructure.

Layer 01 — Foundation

Structured Cabling

Clean Cat6, Cat6A, and fiber pathways with labeled terminations, rack organization, and documentation so every system has a reliable backbone.

  • Cat6 and Cat6A pathways for cameras, readers, network, and AV endpoints
  • Fiber backbone runs where the design calls for them
  • Labeled patch panels, wall plates, and rack terminations
  • Documentation prepared for future service and expansion

Layer 02 — Backbone

Network / PoE

PoE switching, VLAN planning, Wi-Fi coverage, firewall coordination, and remote access design so cameras, readers, and AV devices stay online.

  • PoE switching sized around connected devices
  • Network separation planning for cameras, access, guest Wi-Fi, and business traffic
  • Wireless access point placement for usable coverage
  • Remote management baseline for easier troubleshooting

Cameras

Commercial IP camera layouts planned around entrances, docks, parking lots, counters, and high-value areas — with recording, remote viewing, and coverage strategy.

  • Camera placement planned around real coverage needs
  • Recording and remote viewing configured through the selected platform
  • Indoor and outdoor camera options for facility conditions
  • Optional integration with access events where the platform supports it

Access Control
