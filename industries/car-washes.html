Car Wash Security Camera & Low-Voltage Systems in Metro Detroit

Axonix helps car washes across Metro Detroit improve visibility, payment-area security, equipment-area monitoring, network reliability, and exterior lighting with commercial low-voltage systems.

Common Risks

  • Limited camera coverage around bays, pay stations, vacuums, and entrances
  • Outdoor equipment exposed to weather, moisture, and temperature changes
  • Network issues affecting cameras, payment systems, or remote access
  • Poor lighting around customer areas, equipment rooms, or exterior paths
  • Messy wiring or exposed connections
  • Difficulty reviewing incidents after damage, disputes, or after-hours activity

Recommended Systems

  • Outdoor IP cameras for bays, pay stations, entrances, exits, and vacuum areas
  • Network infrastructure for cameras, payment areas, Wi-Fi, and remote access
  • Structured cabling planned around weather exposure and equipment rooms
  • Access control for offices, equipment rooms, and restricted areas
  • Low-voltage LED lighting for bays, signs, walkways, and exterior visibility
  • Audio or paging systems where needed

Typical Scope

Site walk, weather/exposure review, camera placement plan, network/cabling design, outdoor wiring protection plan, installation, testing, remote access setup, and documentation.

Axonix understands that car wash systems must handle outdoor exposure, customer activity, and long-term serviceability.
