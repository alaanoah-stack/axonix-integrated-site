Office Access Control, Cameras & Low-Voltage Systems in Metro Detroit

Axonix helps Metro Detroit offices improve entry control, network reliability, conference room usability, camera coverage, and lighting with cleanly planned low-voltage systems.

Common Risks

  • Uncontrolled access to offices, server closets, storage rooms, or tenant areas
  • Poor conference room AV usability
  • Weak Wi-Fi or messy network cabling
  • Limited camera coverage for entrances, hallways, or shared areas
  • Poor task, under-cabinet, shelf, or accent lighting
  • Lack of documentation for future moves, adds, and changes

Recommended Systems

  • Access control for entry doors, office suites, server rooms, and restricted areas
  • IP cameras for entrances, lobbies, hallways, and exterior areas
  • Structured cabling for desks, conference rooms, Wi-Fi, cameras, and AV
  • Network infrastructure and Wi-Fi access points
  • Conference room AV and display connectivity
  • Low-voltage LED lighting for task, accent, under-cabinet, and common areas

Typical Scope

Office walk-through, user/workflow review, network and cabling plan, door and camera plan, conference room review, lighting review, installation, testing, documentation, and handoff.

Why Axonix

Axonix designs office systems around usability, clean cabling, reliable network design, and documentation.
