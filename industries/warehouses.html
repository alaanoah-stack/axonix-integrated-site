Warehouse Security & Low-Voltage Systems in Metro Detroit

Axonix helps Metro Detroit warehouses improve visibility, security, communication, and infrastructure reliability with integrated camera systems, access control, structured cabling, network infrastructure, audio/paging, and low-voltage lighting.

Common Risks

  • Blind spots around loading docks, overhead doors, and parking areas
  • Unauthorized access to inventory, offices, mechanical rooms, or IT spaces
  • Poor Wi-Fi or network reliability across large buildings
  • Messy cabling that makes troubleshooting difficult
  • Poor lighting around aisles, exterior doors, and employee entrances
  • Lack of documentation after installation

Recommended Systems

  • IP camera systems for docks, entrances, aisles, and exterior areas
  • Access control for offices, inventory areas, and restricted doors
  • Structured cabling for cameras, access readers, network drops, and AV
  • PoE network infrastructure and Wi-Fi access points
  • Paging and audio systems for announcements and operational communication
  • Low-voltage LED lighting for walkways, work areas, and exterior visibility

Typical Scope

Site walk, coverage planning, cable pathway planning, equipment layout, installation, testing, labeling, documentation, and handoff.

Why Axonix

Axonix plans the infrastructure first, not just the devices. We focus on cabling, network stability, device placement, documentation, and long-term serviceability.
