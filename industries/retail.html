Retail Security Camera & Low-Voltage Systems in Metro Detroit

Axonix helps Metro Detroit retail businesses improve visibility, loss prevention, network reliability, customer experience, and staff usability with integrated security and low-voltage systems.

Common Risks

  • Blind spots around checkout counters, entrances, stockrooms, and parking areas
  • Weak camera coverage for loss prevention and incident review
  • Poor network reliability for POS, cameras, Wi-Fi, and security devices
  • Messy cabling behind counters or displays
  • Poor display, shelf, under-cabinet, or accent lighting
  • No clear documentation when systems need service

Recommended Systems

  • IP cameras for entrances, counters, aisles, stockrooms, and exterior areas
  • Access control for staff-only areas, stockrooms, and office doors
  • Structured cabling for POS, cameras, Wi-Fi, displays, and network devices
  • Network infrastructure and Wi-Fi planning
  • Audio systems for background music or paging
  • Low-voltage LED lighting for shelves, displays, counters, and accent areas

Typical Scope

Retail site assessment, camera and network plan, counter/display cabling plan, lighting review, installation scheduling, system testing, documentation, and handoff.

Why Axonix

Axonix focuses on clean installation, reliable infrastructure, and systems that support daily retail operations.
