Multi-Site Security & Low-Voltage Systems in Metro Detroit

Axonix helps multi-site businesses standardize security and low-voltage infrastructure across multiple locations with repeatable designs, consistent documentation, and scalable support.

Common Risks

  • Different systems installed differently at each location
  • Inconsistent camera coverage, access control rules, or network setup
  • No standard documentation across sites
  • Difficult remote support and troubleshooting
  • Inconsistent user experience for managers and staff
  • Hard-to-scale cabling, network, AV, or lighting standards

Recommended Systems

  • Standardized IP camera layouts and recording platforms
  • Access control standards for doors, schedules, and credentials
  • Structured cabling standards for cameras, network, POS, AV, and lighting
  • Network infrastructure standards for PoE, Wi-Fi, VLANs, and remote access
  • Commercial AV or paging standards where needed
  • Low-voltage LED lighting standards for exterior, interior, signage, or accent areas

Typical Scope

Review existing locations, define a standard system template, document approved equipment and layouts, deploy site by site, test each location, and provide consistent closeout documentation.

Why Axonix

Axonix helps multi-site businesses avoid one-off installs by creating repeatable systems that are easier to manage, support, and expand.
