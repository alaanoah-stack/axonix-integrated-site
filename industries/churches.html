Church Security, Access Control & AV Systems in Metro Detroit

Axonix helps churches and religious facilities across Metro Detroit improve safety, entry control, audio/AV reliability, network infrastructure, and lighting while keeping systems simple for staff and volunteers.

Common Risks

  • Unmonitored entrances, classrooms, offices, and parking areas
  • Volunteer access that is difficult to manage with physical keys
  • Poor camera coverage around doors and exterior areas
  • Audio or AV systems that are hard for volunteers to operate
  • Weak network infrastructure supporting cameras, AV, and Wi-Fi
  • Dark walkways, steps, exterior doors, or parking areas

Recommended Systems

  • IP camera systems for entrances, parking lots, hallways, and common areas
  • Access control for offices, classrooms, storage rooms, and exterior doors
  • Structured cabling and network infrastructure for security and AV devices
  • Commercial audio/AV systems for paging, overflow spaces, and daily use
  • Low-voltage LED lighting for entrances, steps, exterior paths, and accent areas
  • Documentation and handoff designed for staff and volunteer continuity

Typical Scope

Facility walk-through, discussion with leadership, camera and door planning, AV and network review, installation schedule planning around services/events, system testing, and staff handoff.

Why Axonix

Axonix designs church systems to be reliable, documented, and easy for staff or volunteers to understand.
