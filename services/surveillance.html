Commercial Camera System Installation in Metro Detroit

Axonix designs and installs commercial IP camera systems, NVRs, remote viewing, and surveillance infrastructure for warehouses, churches, retail, offices, and multi-site facilities across Metro Detroit.

Problems We Solve

  • Blind spots in warehouse docks, parking lots, or high-value storage areas
  • Outdated analog systems with poor resolution or limited remote access
  • Cameras that fail in extreme Michigan weather conditions
  • Recording systems that crash or lose footage during critical events
  • Difficulty reviewing footage quickly during incidents

What Axonix Installs

  • Commercial-grade IP cameras (indoor, outdoor, PTZ, fisheye)
  • Network Video Recorders (NVRs) with redundant storage options
  • PoE switches and network infrastructure for camera power/data
  • Remote viewing setup for mobile devices and desktops
  • AI analytics: motion detection, line crossing, people counting
  • Integration with access control and alarm systems

How Axonix Designs It

Every camera system starts with a site assessment. We map coverage zones around entrances, docks, parking lots, counters, and high-value areas. We plan cable pathways, PoE requirements, and network segmentation before installation begins. After deployment, we test every camera angle, recording function, and remote access feature before handoff.
