Security System Monitoring & Support in Metro Detroit

Ongoing documentation, remote support options, and maintenance planning so your security and low-voltage systems remain manageable and reliable after installation across Metro Detroit.

Problems We Solve

  • Systems that become difficult to manage after the original installer leaves
  • Lack of documentation for troubleshooting, expansion, or staff turnover
  • Unexpected downtime with no dedicated support contact
  • Difficulty adding new devices, cameras, or users to existing systems
  • Security gaps from unpatched firmware or outdated network configurations

What Axonix Provides

  • Complete as-built documentation: wiring diagrams, labels, credentials, IP schemes
  • Remote support options for configuration changes and troubleshooting
  • Preventive maintenance plans: firmware updates, hardware health checks
  • System optimization reviews as your facility requirements evolve
  • Hands-on training sessions for facilities or IT staff
  • Priority response agreements for critical operational issues

How Axonix Designs It

Support starts at handoff. We organize system documentation so your team understands exactly how everything connects. We offer remote assistance for common issues and on-site visits for hardware needs. We schedule periodic reviews to ensure your cameras, access readers, network switches, and AV systems evolve with your business instead of falling behind.
