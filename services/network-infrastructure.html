Commercial Network Infrastructure in Metro Detroit

PoE switching, VLAN planning, Wi-Fi coverage, firewall coordination, and remote access design so cameras, readers, and AV devices stay online across Metro Detroit facilities.

Problems We Solve

  • Cameras or access readers dropping offline due to PoE limitations
  • Network congestion affecting security system performance
  • Wi-Fi dead zones in large facilities or outdoor areas
  • Security devices sharing the same network as guest or public traffic
  • Difficulty managing remote access securely for staff and vendors

What Axonix Installs

  • PoE+ and PoE++ switches sized for camera and access device loads
  • VLAN segmentation for security, guest, and operational traffic
  • Commercial Wi-Fi access points with predictive site survey planning
  • Firewall rules and secure remote access configuration
  • Network monitoring and alerting setup for proactive maintenance
  • Redundant internet failover options for critical operations

How Axonix Designs It

We inventory your existing network equipment and bandwidth requirements before making recommendations. We design VLANs to isolate security traffic, size PoE budgets for current and future devices, and plan Wi-Fi coverage with predictive modeling. After deployment, we document IP schemes, credentials, and management procedures so your team maintains full control.
