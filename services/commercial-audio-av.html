Commercial Audio & AV Systems in Metro Detroit

Commercial paging, background audio, conference room AV, and multi-zone sound systems designed to be simple for staff and reliable for daily use across Metro Detroit facilities.

Problems We Solve

  • Inconsistent audio coverage in large warehouses or retail spaces
  • Confusing AV controls that staff avoid using
  • Paging systems that fail during emergencies or daily operations
  • Conference rooms with poor microphone pickup or video quality
  • Multiple disconnected audio systems requiring separate management

What Axonix Installs

  • Commercial paging amplifiers and ceiling/wall speakers
  • Background music systems with zone control and scheduling
  • Conference room AV: displays, microphone arrays, control systems
  • Multi-zone audio with source selection and independent volume control
  • Integration with fire alarm systems for emergency paging
  • Simple touch-panel or mobile app controls for daily use

How Axonix Designs It

We assess acoustic challenges, ambient noise levels, and usage patterns for each space. We plan speaker placement for even coverage, design intuitive control interfaces, and coordinate AV cabling with your existing network. After installation, we train staff and provide quick-reference guides so the system is actually used instead of ignored.
