Structured Cabling Installation in Metro Detroit

Professional structured cabling for Metro Detroit commercial facilities. Cat6, Cat6A, fiber pathways, labeled terminations, and organized rack infrastructure built for reliability.

Problems We Solve

  • Disorganized cabling causing network downtime or difficult troubleshooting
  • Insufficient cable capacity for future technology upgrades
  • Poor labeling leading to confusion during moves, adds, or changes
  • Cables run through ceilings or walls without proper pathways or protection
  • Mixed cable grades creating performance bottlenecks

What Axonix Installs

  • Category 6, 6A, and fiber optic cabling with proper bend radius
  • Labeled patch panels, keystone jacks, and commercial wall plates
  • Organized equipment racks with vertical and horizontal cable management
  • Conduit, J-hooks, and cable trays for clean, code-compliant pathways
  • Fluke testing and certification documentation for every drop
  • As-built diagrams and labeling schematics for facility handoff

How Axonix Designs It

We survey your facility to plan cable pathways that avoid electrical interference and allow for future expansion. We specify cable grades based on bandwidth requirements and distance limits. Every termination is labeled at both ends, and we provide complete documentation so your team can manage changes confidently without guessing.
