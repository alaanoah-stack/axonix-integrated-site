Access Control Systems for Metro Detroit Businesses

Professional access control installation for Metro Detroit facilities. Door hardware, credential management, audit trails, and network-integrated security from Axonix Integrated Technologies.

Problems We Solve

  • Unsecured entry points vulnerable to unauthorized access
  • Lost or shared keys creating security gaps
  • No audit trail of who entered which areas and when
  • Manual door management consuming staff time
  • Inability to quickly revoke access for terminated employees

What Axonix Installs

  • Card readers, keypads, and mobile credential readers
  • Electric strikes, magnetic locks, and door controllers
  • Access control software with user management and scheduling
  • Integration with cameras for visual verification
  • Emergency override protocols and fire alarm integration
  • Cloud-based or on-premise system options

How Axonix Designs It

We start by mapping your facility's access points and security zones. We coordinate door hardware with your existing infrastructure, plan network requirements for controllers, and design credential workflows that match your operations. After installation, we train your staff and provide documentation for ongoing management.
